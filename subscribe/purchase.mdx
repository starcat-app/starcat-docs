## What this is for

Unlock Pro. Entitlements match across channels. Receipts do not. Confirm whether you installed MAS or Direct first.

## How to buy

| Channel | Best for | Entry |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Mac App Store** | Apple ID subscriptions | [App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12) |
| **Direct** | DMG from [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) | Site pricing, or in-app **Settings → Pro** |

Prices follow the purchase page (site reference: $3.99 / month, $29.99 / year, $39.99 Lifetime). There is no separate trial. Free already includes sync, capped tags, and search.

**App Store:** install from the store → **Settings → Pro** → pick a plan → Apple ID / StoreKit → entitlements apply immediately.

**Direct:** starcat.ink or **Settings → Pro** → checkout → License on this device (seats: [License](/subscribe/license)).

After purchase: Pro unlocks immediately; sidebar / settings show Pro; subscriptions show an end date; Lifetime stays valid within seat rules.

## Notes

- The wrong channel looks like “I paid but cannot restore”. MAS uses Restore; Direct uses a License.
- Direct Lifetime is at most 2 Macs for personal use.
- Confirm channel and live prices before paying. Next: [Plans](/subscribe/plan-comparison), [Restore](/subscribe/restore).
