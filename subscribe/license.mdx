## Purpose

Direct Pro is a License. App Store Pro is an Apple subscription / Lifetime transaction. Mixing the two when moving Macs leads to "seats full" or a Restore that does nothing.

## Two grant types

| | Mac App Store | Direct |
|--|---------------|--------|
| Pay | Apple / StoreKit | Site checkout |
| Proof | Apple subscription / Lifetime | License |
| New Mac | Restore + same Apple ID | Unlink old device, then activate |
| Devices | Apple’s rules | Lifetime and similar: 2 Macs personal use |

Direct: the License usually binds this device after purchase. Settings → Pro → License shows status and devices. Unlink to free a seat. A full seat list blocks Pro on a new Mac.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Valid | Pro works |
| Expired | Monthly / yearly not renewed |
| Seats full | Device cap reached |
| Unlinked | This Mac no longer authorized |

Direct offline: entitlements restore locally at launch, with periodic silent checks. Failures or expiry ask you to re-verify.

App Store users do not manage a License. Same Apple ID + [Restore](/subscribe/restore).

## Notes

Receipts do not cross channels.

Unlink the old Mac only after you are done with it, or both machines can drop Pro.
