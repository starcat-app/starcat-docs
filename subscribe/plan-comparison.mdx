## Pricing plans

Starcat offers a free tier and Pro. Free covers Stars organization and search; Pro unlocks AI, the knowledge base, higher limits, and more.

<Note>
  Prices follow [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) and in-app purchase screens. If this page disagrees with checkout, **checkout wins**.
</Note>

## Pro pricing

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Pro Monthly** | $3.99 / mo | Auto-renews monthly |
| **Pro Yearly** | $29.99 / yr | Auto-renews yearly (saves vs monthly) |

## Where to buy

- **Mac App Store**: [Starcat for GitHub](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12)

Pro entitlements match across channels. See [Buy Pro](/subscribe/purchase).

## BYOK mode

Pro users configure their provider API key under **Settings → AI**:

- Use your own API quota  
- Requests go to your configured provider  
- Switch models flexibly  

See [BYOK mode](/subscribe/byok).

## How to choose a channel

| You care most about… | Suggestion |
|----------------------|------------|
| Apple ID billing and Family Sharing rules | [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12) |
| Website Lifetime / Direct install | [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) |
| Two Macs long term | Lifetime (Direct seat rules in [License](/subscribe/license)) |
| Try one year first | Yearly |

Pro entitlements are **identical** across channels; purchase receipts **do not** transfer. See [Restore purchase](/subscribe/restore).
