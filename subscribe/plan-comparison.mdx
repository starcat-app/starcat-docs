## Purpose

Free already covers Stars tidy, search, and explore. Pro unlocks AI, the knowledge base, and higher caps. App Store and Direct match on entitlements; pick the channel that matches how you install. Prices follow [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) and the in-app purchase page. If they disagree, the purchase page wins.

## Pricing and channels

| Plan | Price | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Pro Monthly | $3.99 / month | Auto-renew |
| Pro Yearly | $29.99 / year | Auto-renew |
| Pro Lifetime | $39.99 | One-time; up to 2 Macs for personal use |

- Mac App Store: [Starcat for GitHub](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12)
- Direct: [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink)

Pro entitlements match across channels. Receipts do not. See [Purchase](/subscribe/purchase) and [Restore](/subscribe/restore).

Apple ID / family sharing → App Store. Direct DMG / Lifetime seat → starcat.ink. Two Macs long-term → Lifetime ([License](/subscribe/license)). Try a year → Yearly.

AI calls use your own key. See [BYOK](/subscribe/byok).

## Notes

App Store and Direct are separate installs. License and StoreKit cannot restore each other.

Direct can launch local CLIs / Agent runtimes. App Store is sandboxed. See [GitHub connection](/getting-started/github-connection).

Cloud sync and JSON import/export are not shipped. Pro is not a cloud drive.
