## Purpose

Explore has two reading surfaces. Neither is your Stars list. Daily briefings are themed reports. Weekly AI events are the latest issues plus fact-check editions.

## Daily briefings

Sidebar **Explore → Daily**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a topic">
    The sidebar shows a count per category. Turn categories off in Settings. Names follow the app language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read this issue or an older one">
    Use the issue calendar. Covers show a placeholder while loading. Reports stay readable offline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share only if you mean to">
    Turn the report and cover into a public link, then copy it or use the system share sheet. Existing links come back after restart, and sharing again reuses the same link. See [Share links](/share-export/share-links).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Weekly AI events

Sidebar **Explore → Weekly AI events**. Read the latest six issues and fact-check editions, play the summary, load article images on demand, and open the source or the related repository. Read items stay available offline.

## Watch-outs

- Briefing text follows the app language, not the repository README language.
- A shared report is a public link. Do not put private notes into a report and then share it.
