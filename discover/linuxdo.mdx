## The job

You want vetted open-source projects that people in the Chinese community are already using, not another pass through English Trending.

The Linux.do column is that list. App Store 1.9.1 and Direct 1.9.6 both have it.

## How to use it

1. Sidebar **Explore → Linux.do**. Browse by language subcategory.
2. In other repository lists, a Linux.do source mark means the row came from here.
3. Open the detail and use the source tag to open the original thread in the browser.

Source preferences stick across launches, same as Explore and Weekly. See [Explore overview](/discover/explore/overview).

## Watch-outs

The list is vetted projects only. It is not every thread on Linux.do.
