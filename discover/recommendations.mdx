## The job

You do not want to describe a need. You want a few repositories worth opening today, based on what you already starred.

<DirectVersionBadge /> Daily recommendations are those cards. App Store 1.9.1 does not have them yet. A model-written reason still requires Pro.

## How to use it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open today's cards">
    Use the recommendations entry. Ask for another batch. Automatic display is a setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Act on a card">
    Open the source, Star it, or mark it not interested. A successful Star clears that not-interested mark.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revisit history">
    History is ordered by the first day a project was recommended. Search it, or filter by feedback.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Watch-outs

Cards and history do not require Pro. A model-written reason does. If that reason fails, the card and source link still work.

Public Star contribution is off by default and is a separate choice. See [Privacy](/data-privacy/privacy).
