## The job

You may be on the store build or the website build. The same sentence can be a button on one side and a missing entry on the other. Check which build you installed before you follow a feature page.

Shipped versions: Mac App Store **1.9.1**, Direct **1.9.6**.

Pages without a capsule exist on both. A capsule means one channel owns it, or only one shipped version has it today.

<AppStoreBadge /> Store sandbox or Apple purchase only.

<DirectBadge /> Website build only. The store sandbox cannot take the same path, including after a later update.

<DirectVersionBadge /> Direct 1.9.6 has it. The App Store build is still 1.9.1 and does not.
