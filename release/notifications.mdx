## What they are for

When a subscribed repo ships a Release and polling sees it, you may get a macOS banner and unread marks on the timeline / Activity. A notification is a reminder. You still download assets yourself.

## Checklist

| Check | Where |
|-------|-------|
| Subscribed | [Subscriptions](/release/subscription) |
| Under the free cap of 5 (or Pro) | [Plans](/subscribe/plan-comparison) |
| Notification permission allowed | First-subscribe dialog; System Settings later |
| In-app Release notification switch | **Settings → General → Notifications** |
| Background polling healthy | [Background refresh](/release/background-refresh) |

Content usually includes repo name, tag, and Release title. A click jumps to that Release / timeline.

To quiet things: disable system banners, or the in-app Release switch, or unsubscribe low-priority repos to free slots.

## Notes

- No system permission means no banner. Timeline unread can still appear.
- Clicking a system notification often lands on the notification timeline or the Release timeline. See [Activity](/discover/activity/activity).
