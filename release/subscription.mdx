## What it is for

After you subscribe, Starcat polls that repo’s GitHub Releases, updates the [timeline](/release/timeline), and can send [system notifications](/release/notifications). Use the free 5 slots on libraries whose versions actually affect you, not every casual Star.

<Note>
  Free: **5** repos. Pro: unlimited. See [Plans](/subscribe/plan-comparison).
</Note>

## How to subscribe

1. Open detail: the repo must be starred **or** ingested
2. Tap **Subscribe to Releases**
3. The first time, macOS may ask for notification permission; allow it if you want banners

| State | Can subscribe |
|-------|----------------|
| Starred | Yes |
| Not starred, ingested | Yes |
| Neither | No |

If the association is gone, a historical subscription may remain but polling pauses → [Background refresh](/release/background-refresh).

Unsubscribe from detail or the subscription list to free a slot. Manage from the sidebar Release timeline or Activity → Release.

## Notes

- No Star and no ingest means you cannot subscribe.
- Hitting 5 on free is gated. Drop a low-priority repo or upgrade Pro.
- Subscribe does not download installers. See [Assets](/release/assets-download).
