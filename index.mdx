## Five-minute tour

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and connect GitHub">
    [Install](/getting-started/installation) → [Connect GitHub](/getting-started/github-connection) → tap Sync
  </Step>
  <Step title="Three-column workspace">
    Sidebar filters → list → README / notes. [Main interface](/main-interface/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize and find">
    [Tags](/manage-stars/tags) · [Notes & status](/manage-stars/notes-and-status) · [⌘K Search Center](/search/search-center)
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI and knowledge base">
    [AI overview](/ai-features/overview) (Pro) → heart to ingest → [RAG workspace](/knowledge-base/overview) → [Agent workspace](/agent-workspace/overview)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track and discover">
    [Release subscriptions](/release/subscription) · [Explore](/discover/explore/overview) · [Activity](/discover/activity/activity) · [Insights](/main-interface/insights)
  </Step>
</Steps>
