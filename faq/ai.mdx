---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="AI entry is locked?">
    Pro is required, and you must configure a provider under Settings → AI. See [Purchase](/subscribe/purchase) and [Configuration](/ai-features/configuration).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Purchased but still locked?">
    App Store: **Settings → Pro → Restore purchase**. Direct: check license and device seats. See [Restore purchase](/subscribe/restore).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Semantic search unavailable?">
    Besides Pro, you must configure **Embedding** and finish indexing. Chat alone is not enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="RAG answers the wrong thing?">
    Confirm the repo is ingested and index coverage looks healthy; use @ to scope repos; check [Citations](/knowledge-base/citations).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Auto Tidy tags randomly?">
    Turn off automation first; review tag confirmation policy and the free 20-tag cap; tighten action scope. See [Auto Tidy](/ai-features/auto-tidy).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Index keeps spinning, or pause reports a network error?">
    Coverage and last prefetch live in [Settings → AI](/settings/ai). Pausing vectorization is not treated as being offline. External Meilisearch / Qdrant each show a green check, orange rebuild warning, or red cross next to **Test and save**. See [Index management](/knowledge-base/index-management).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
