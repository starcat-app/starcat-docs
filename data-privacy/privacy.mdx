## Principles (summary)

Starcat defaults to **local-first**:

- Tags, notes, status, and other user data stay on your Mac first  
- No selling personal data; no ad tracking  
- Starcat does not read your GitHub password  
- AI keys live in Keychain; notes are not used to “train our models”  

Full legal text: [starcat.ink/privacy](https://starcat.ink/privacy).

## Data categories

| Type | Examples | Rebuildable from GitHub? |
|------|----------|--------------------------|
| Repo cache | Metadata, README | Yes |
| User data | Tags, notes, status, collection rules | **No** |
| Secrets | GitHub token, AI keys | Keychain |

Cross-device cloud sync is not shipped → [Backup](/data-privacy/backup).

## AI and third parties

- **BYOK**: requests go to your configured provider → [BYOK](/subscribe/byok)  
- **External search**: queries go to enabled providers only → [External search](/search/external-search)  
- **Sharing**: share cards or public links only when you choose → [Share cards](/share-export/share-cards)  

## Public Star data contribution

**Settings → General → Privacy & Recommendations** has **Anonymously contribute public Star data**, **off by default**. After a successful full Stars sync, Starcat can silently upload public repository IDs and Star times to improve recommendations.

Private repositories, tags, notes, browsing activity, and account identity are never uploaded. Uploads use a random anonymous identifier. Failures do not affect normal sync.

A one-time consent sheet may appear; choose **Not Now** to keep the switch off.

## Telemetry

Anonymous telemetry is **off by default**; enable under **Settings → Diagnostics**. See [Telemetry](/data-privacy/telemetry).
