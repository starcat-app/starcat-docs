## Two authorization models

| | Mac App Store | Direct (website) |
|--|---------------|------------------|
| Payment | Apple / StoreKit | Website checkout |
| Credential | Apple subscription / one-time purchase | License |
| New Mac | **Restore purchase** + same Apple ID | Unbind old device → activate on new device |
| Devices | Follows Apple subscription rules | Lifetime and similar: up to **2** Macs for personal use |

## Direct: activate a license

After purchase, the license usually binds to the current device automatically. You can also check status and the device list under **Settings → Pro → License**.

## Direct: device seats

- View activated devices  
- **Unbind**: free a seat before activating on a new Mac  
- When seats are full, Pro cannot be enabled on another device until you unbind one  

## License status (Direct)

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Pro is available |
| **Expired** | Monthly / Yearly not renewed |
| **Seats full** | Device limit reached |
| **Unbound** | This Mac no longer holds the license |

## Offline use (Direct)

- Entitlements restore from local storage at launch  
- Periodic silent validation  
- Prompts to re-verify when something is wrong or expired  

## App Store users

No manual license management is required. After switching Macs or reinstalling, use the same Apple ID and tap **Restore purchase** under **Settings → Pro**. See [Restore purchase](/en/subscribe/restore).
