## Purchase channels

Starcat Pro **is available now**. Choose either channel:

| Channel | Best for | Entry |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Mac App Store** | Managing subscriptions with your Apple ID | [App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12) |
| **Direct (website)** | Users who installed the DMG from [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) | Pricing on the website, or **Settings → Pro** in the app |

<Note>
  Pro entitlements are the same across both channels. Prices follow the live checkout page (current website reference: Monthly $3.99 / Yearly $29.99 / Lifetime $39.99).
</Note>

## App Store purchase

1. Install Starcat from the [Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/starcat-for-github/id6788809803?mt=12)  
2. Open **Settings → Pro**  
3. Choose **Monthly / Yearly / Lifetime**  
4. Complete StoreKit checkout with your Apple ID  
5. Entitlements take effect immediately  

## Website / Direct purchase

1. Open [starcat.ink](https://starcat.ink) or **Settings → Pro** in the app  
2. Choose a plan and complete checkout  
3. The license activates on the current device (Lifetime and similar plans are subject to device seat limits — see [License management](/en/subscribe/license))  

## After purchase

- Pro features unlock immediately  
- Pro status appears in the sidebar and Settings  
- Subscriptions show the renewal date; Lifetime is permanent (subject to device seat rules)  

## Free trial

There is no separate trial period. The free tier already includes Stars sync, tags (with limits), search, and other basics — use it first, then upgrade when ready.
