## Hot

**Explore → Hot**: a leaderboard of high-heat repositories, usually with language filters.

## New releases

**Explore → New releases**: repos that are new or tied to fresh releases (sort rules follow the app).

## Organize after discovery

When something looks worth keeping:

1. Star (optional)  
2. Tag / write a short note  
3. For long-term use → [Ingest](/en/knowledge-base/adding-repos)
