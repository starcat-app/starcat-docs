## Behavior

AI recommends tags from README / context (often 3–8; confidence may be shown).

**Nothing is written until you confirm**—part of Starcat’s conservative AI policy. See [AI overview](/en/ai-features/overview).

## Entry points

- Tag recommendations in the detail AI panel  
- [Auto Tidy](/en/ai-features/auto-tidy) batch recommendations (still subject to confirm / action settings)  

## Limits

The free tier allows at most **20** tags. If you are at the cap:

1. Delete unused tags, or  
2. Upgrade to [Pro](/en/subscribe/plan-comparison)  

See [Tag management](/en/manage-stars/tags).

## Tips

- Define your naming convention first, then let AI align to it  
- Subtract from recommendations to avoid tag sprawl
