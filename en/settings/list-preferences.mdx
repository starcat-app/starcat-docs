## Overview

How lists sort, what they filter by default, and which languages appear in the pool affects your first view every time you open Manage.

These preferences may appear in:

- **Settings → General** and list-related items  
- Sort and filter controls on the list toolbar itself  

## Languages of interest

Language filter candidates often come from languages that actually appear in your Stars. You can also maintain a smaller “languages of interest” set to keep the dropdown manageable. See [Filters](/en/search/filters).

## Reset

If list behavior looks wrong, use **Reset list preferences** in [General settings](/en/settings/general).
