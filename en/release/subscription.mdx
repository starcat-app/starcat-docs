## What it does

After you subscribe, Starcat polls the repo’s GitHub Releases, updates the [timeline](/en/release/timeline), and optionally sends [system notifications](/en/release/notifications).

<Note>
  Free tier: up to **5** repositories; Pro is unlimited. See [Plan comparison](/en/subscribe/plan-comparison).
</Note>

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open detail">The repo must already be starred or ingested.</Step>
  <Step title="Tap Subscribe">In the detail pane, **Subscribe to Release**.</Step>
  <Step title="Allow notifications">The first time, macOS may ask for permission—allowing is recommended.</Step>
</Steps>

## Eligibility

| State | Can subscribe |
|-------|---------------|
| Starred | ✅ |
| Not starred but ingested | ✅ |
| Neither | ❌ |

Without a valid link, historical subscriptions may remain but polling pauses → [Background refresh](/en/release/background-refresh).

## Unsubscribe

Cancel from detail or the subscription list to free a slot on the free tier.

## Management entry points

- Sidebar **Release timeline**  
- Activity → Release category
