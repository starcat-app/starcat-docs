## How citations appear

Repository citation chips in answers (e.g. `[owner/repo]`) mean chunks from that repo matched. On click:

- Right Inspector shows evidence  
- May auto-switch to the citations pane and scroll  
- Source types can include README, notes, AI summary, etc.  

Keyword hits on **metadata shards** and knowledge-base stats also show the actual quoted fragment, without waiting for vectorization.

## Best practices

| Practice | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Open source for key claims | Avoid over-summary or cross-repo mix-ups |
| Narrow with @ | Cleaner citations |
| Export answers with citations | Easier audit later → [Sessions](/knowledge-base/session-management) |

## No citations?

Possible causes:

- Question too broad; model did not attach sources  
- Index does not cover relevant repos  
- @ scope excluded the real sources  

Try: rephrase → check [index](/knowledge-base/index-management) → @ specific repos.
