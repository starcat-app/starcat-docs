## What they are for

The first look when you open Manage: sort, default filters, how long the language menu is.

Preferences may live under **Settings → General / list items**, or on the list toolbar itself.

Language filter options often come from languages actually present in your Stars. An “interest languages” subset keeps the menu short. See [Filters](/search/filters).

If the list feels stuck, **Reset list preferences** in [General](/settings/general).
