## Defaults (common)

| Action | Default |
|--------|---------|
| Search Center | **⌘K** |
| Focus list search | **⌘F** |
| Repo AI overlay | **⇧⌘A** |
| RAG workspace | **⇧⌘K** |

Follow what Settings shows; changes apply immediately or after restart per app rules.

## Constraints

- Usually requires at least one modifier key plus a letter or number to avoid swallowing normal typing  
- Conflicts with in-app reserved combos are rejected
