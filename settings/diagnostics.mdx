## What it is for

**Settings → Diagnostics** turns anonymous telemetry on (**off by default**) or exports a support pack. Do not attach that pack to a public issue.

Enabled telemetry sends allow-listed events and coarse metrics. See [Telemetry](/data-privacy/telemetry).

Export Diagnostics includes version, database status, and recent errors (redacted). Email [dong4j@gmail.com](mailto:dong4j@gmail.com).
