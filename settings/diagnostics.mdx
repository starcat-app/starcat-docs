## Entry

**Settings → Diagnostics**

## Anonymous telemetry

**Off by default.** When enabled, only whitelisted events and coarse metrics are sent. Details: [Telemetry and diagnostics](/data-privacy/telemetry).

## Export diagnostics

**Export Diagnostics** in the menu (wording follows the app) exports version, database status, recent errors, and more (sensitive fields redacted). Email to [dong4j@gmail.com](mailto:dong4j@gmail.com).
