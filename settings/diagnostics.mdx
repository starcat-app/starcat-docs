## The job

Something broke and you want support to see it, without also sending repository contents, prompts, or keys.

Diagnostics leave anonymous telemetry off. The exported pack has the version, database status, and redacted recent errors. Do not attach it to a public issue.

Enabled telemetry sends allow-listed events and coarse metrics. See [Telemetry](/data-privacy/telemetry).

Export Diagnostics includes version, database status, and recent errors (redacted). Email [dong4j@gmail.com](mailto:dong4j@gmail.com).

## Enhanced recording <DirectVersionBadge />

The bug comes and goes, and you need a recording that includes your own description, not an empty crash log.

In **Settings → Diagnostics**, write the problem, the area, and when it happened, then turn on enhanced recording. It runs for at most 15 minutes. Exporting the report is a separate action. Logs stay on this Mac. App Store 1.9.1 does not have enhanced recording yet. Ordinary export exists on both.

The menu-bar status panel (left click) also shows sync and background work. See [Toolbar](/main-interface/toolbar).
