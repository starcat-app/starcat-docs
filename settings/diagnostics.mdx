## Purpose

Anonymous telemetry is off by default. Settings → Diagnostics can turn it on, or export a support pack. Do not attach that pack to a public issue.

Enabled telemetry sends allow-listed events and coarse metrics. See [Telemetry](/data-privacy/telemetry).

Export Diagnostics includes version, database status, and recent errors (redacted). Email [dong4j@gmail.com](mailto:dong4j@gmail.com).

## Enhanced recording

For a bug that is hard to reproduce, describe the problem, the area, and when it happened in **Settings → Diagnostics**, then turn on enhanced recording. It runs for at most 15 minutes. Exporting the report is a separate action. Logs stay on this Mac. Do not paste them into a public issue.

The menu-bar status panel (left click) also shows sync and background work. See [Toolbar](/main-interface/toolbar).
