## What it is for

Before reading a long README, grab structured highlights (background, features, fit, caveats; the shape follows your prompt). Good for triaging new Stars. Not a license or security verdict.

## How to open it

1. Detail AI overlay → Summary (**⇧⌘A**)
2. [Auto Tidy](/ai-features/auto-tidy) for batch generation

Requires Pro and a working Chat model.

## Cache

Summaries are cached locally so you do not burn tokens twice. Clear them in **Settings → Storage**. See [Storage](/settings/storage). Regenerating calls the model again; cost follows your provider.

## Notes

- Does not replace reading the license, security advisories, or the latest Release
- Very short or image-only READMEs produce weak summaries
- Auto Tidy batch generation can spike the bill; confirm quality on one repo first
