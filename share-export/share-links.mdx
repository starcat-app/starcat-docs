## What they are for

Copy an address like `https://starcat.ink/r/{owner}/{repo}`. If the other person has Starcat installed, the link should **launch the app and select that repo**. Use it in chat. It is not a PNG export.

## How to use them

1. Open the repo in Manage, Explore, or My projects
2. Use Share / Copy link in detail or the toolbar (copy follows the app)
3. Open the URL on another Mac. Universal Links / associated domains should hand off to Starcat

| Shape | Use |
|-------|-----|
| Public HTTPS | `https://starcat.ink/r/owner/repo?v=1` (optional `rid` for a stable repo id) |
| Local App URL | `starcat://repo/owner/repo` (widgets and local integrations) |

Older links without `rid` still resolve by `owner/name`.

AI-assisted share content can **reuse an existing AI summary**, and you can cancel or regenerate the share link in the UI.

## Notes

- Without Starcat installed, the link stays on the web.
- `starcat://` only means something on this Mac. See [Desktop launchers](/integrations/desktop-launchers).
