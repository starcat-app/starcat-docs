## Entry

Sidebar user area **Share** → opens the share card editor.

## Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick layout and colors">Multiple visual styles (dashboard, terminal, etc. — follow the in-app list).</Step>
  <Step title="Preview">Confirm stats and layout.</Step>
  <Step title="Export">Save PNG, copy to clipboard, or share to X (per available buttons).</Step>
</Steps>

Shows your Stars stats as a personal card — **not** HTML/Markdown export of the repo list.

## vs file export

| | Share card | File export |
|--|------------|-------------|
| Output | Image | HTML / Markdown files |
| Entry | May coexist in the same share sheet | [Export files](/share-export/export-files) |
